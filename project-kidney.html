01 / THE QUESTION

Which CNN backbone works best for multi-class kidney ultrasound, and can we see where it looks?

02 / THE APPROACH

  • Compared CNN, VGG16, ResNet50, InceptionV3 and EfficientNetB3
  • Grad-CAM to check the model attends to the kidney region
  • Flask dashboard to upload an image and see the prediction
